Dispatcher · orders

Create an order

  1. Sign in on the dispatcher page with your assigned phone number and password. The +993 prefix stays fixed. Enter the eight local digits.
  2. Open Заказы and choose Создать заказ.
  3. Choose the region, enter the customer phone number and a positive price in TMT. These fields are required.
  4. Add a pickup address, destination, map point or note if you know them. They are optional.
  5. Choose a public announcement or a private driver, then send the order. For a private offer, a selected driver is required.

Public announcement

  1. First 20 secondsOnly drivers in the order’s saved region can claim.
  2. Then all regionsIf nobody claims, all eligible drivers can claim themselves.
  3. First claim winsThe order disappears for everyone else.

Private offer

  1. Select a driverOnline, free, fresh GPS, enough balance.
  2. 20-second offerThe selected driver must confirm.
  3. Expiry choiceOpen to all regions, or stop unassigned.
Choose whether to offer the order to other drivers after the private deadline. When ON, all regions open at once. There is no second 20-second timer. When OFF, the order remains unassigned for the dispatcher to handle.

An early private decline returns the order to its original region until the original deadline. The expiry choice then applies. The customer phone is hidden until acceptance.

Dispatcher · monitoring

Find a driver and follow the trip

  1. Open the monitoring map. Search the roster by name, phone or vehicle plate. Filter by region or status. On mobile, open the driver list as a bottom sheet.
  2. Select a driver from a map marker or roster row. Check the location age, online state and whether the driver is free.
  3. Use Предложить заказ for an eligible driver. Choose an existing waiting order or create a new private order.
  4. Follow the assigned order through pickup, waiting and the trip. A last known point is not a live position.
What you seeWhat it means

Drivers and balances

Create drivers in the driver list. Use a valid +993 phone number. A six-digit numeric password is allowed. When changing a phone or resetting a password, give the new credentials to the driver privately. Previous access is revoked and the driver signs in again.

Top up the pilot balance before assigning work. A driver needs the full order price to accept. For example, 15 TMT cannot cover a 30 TMT order. The app prevents a negative balance.

A started trip older than 15 minutes is marked as needing completion. Contact the driver first. If the trip is stuck, use the dispatcher resolve action and enter the reason. The app records the action; it does not complete trips automatically.

Driver · shift

Get ready and go online

  1. Open the driver app and sign in with your assigned phone number and password.
  2. Allow precise location and notifications when asked. Android system location must also be switched on.
  3. Check the location and connection indicator. If it is still checking, wait for a fresh position and dispatcher connection. If there is a warning, read its message.
  4. Press the Online switch. The app confirms the shift with the dispatcher before showing that you are live.
  5. Leave the app running during your shift. When finished, press Offline.
  1. Location readyPermission, system GPS and a fresh position.
  2. Connection readyThe app can reach the dispatcher.
  3. Online confirmedThe dispatcher can offer new work.
SituationWhat to do
Location disabledEnable location permission and Android system location, then return to the app.
No internetRestore Wi-Fi or mobile data. GPS alone cannot send updates or accept work.
Outside every regionYour real location stays visible as outside region. You may claim orders once they are opened to all regions.
Address missingUse the region and coordinates. An address lookup failure never blocks a shift.
Offline can retain a last known position. During an active shift, the app sends fresh positions. During a lost connection, new work stops after the server’s availability grace period. An accepted trip stays assigned.

On the target Android phone, background delivery must still be checked with the screen locked and battery restrictions enabled. A closed browser does not provide reliable background alerts.

Driver · orders

From acceptance to completion

  1. AcceptChoose arrival time and confirm. Price is charged once.
  2. At pickupPress Я на месте and wait for the customer.
  3. Customer boardedPress Начать поездку.
  4. Trip finishedPress Завершить поездку.
  1. The home grid shows every saved region. Each card shows cars and orders. Open a region to view its available orders.
  2. A new regional or private offer can appear automatically. A private offer has a fixed 20-second deadline. Decline it if you cannot take it.
  3. Check the price and arrival time. Accept only when you can carry out the trip. A customer phone appears after acceptance; use it to confirm pickup.
  4. Use the current trip controls on the home screen. Open the trip details for extra information or permitted cancellation.
  5. Complete the trip once it is finished. Completion does not charge the price again.
If another driver claims first, the order disappears. If you try at the same moment, the app reports that it is already taken. Only one driver can own the order.

Balance and cancellation

You need enough balance for the full price. With 30 TMT, you can accept a 30 TMT order. With 15 TMT, a 30 TMT order can be visible but cannot be accepted.

Driver cancellation is intended only while waiting for the customer. After starting the trip, cancellation is unavailable. A permitted cancellation refunds the original charge once. Do not repeatedly accept and cancel to test a live customer order.

If you lose internet, keep the accepted trip in mind and reconnect. Do not create another account or accept a second trip. If the trip cannot be completed in the app, contact the dispatcher.

Both roles · communication

Chat and recovery

  1. Open the conversation with the dispatcher or selected driver. Type a message and press Send. Enter sends; Shift+Enter adds a line.
  2. Read older messages inside the chat area. The app keeps your reading position when you scroll upward.
  3. If a send fails, the draft stays available. Retry it. The same retry does not create a duplicate message.
  4. After reconnecting or returning to the app, allow it to recover missed messages and the current order state.
  1. Connection lostThe last displayed information may be old.
  2. ReconnectThe app checks current server state.
  3. Work continuesThe accepted trip and balance remain consistent.

Dispatcher · daily view

Read the pilot results

The Today panel follows Asia/Ashgabat time. It groups orders by the date they were created. Live waiting orders are counted separately, including orders left from yesterday.

MeasureHow to read it
Waiting orders and oldest waitThe work still available now. Active private reservations are separate. Stopped private orders are not public waiting work.
Median / p90 acceptance timeTypical waiting time and the time within which 90% of measured orders were accepted. Check the sample count.
Completed / cancelledOutcomes for orders created today. Unfinished orders are shown separately. Rates use completed plus cancelled orders.

Before the phone pilot

  1. Use the freshly built APK and the matching deployed service. An older installed APK may show older screens.
  2. Test one public order, one private acceptance, one expiry and two drivers claiming the same order. Confirm only one wins.
  3. Check the price charge, permitted cancellation refund and normal trip completion.
  4. Test GPS permission changes, screen lock, background delivery, Wi-Fi/mobile switching and a connection loss.
  5. Keep the phone model, Android version and observed result with the test record.
This is a pilot guide. Production signing, update distribution and physical Infinix Android 14 background reliability still need release verification.
