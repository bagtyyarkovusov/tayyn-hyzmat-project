Dispatcher · orders

Create an order

  1. Sign in on the dispatcher page with your assigned phone number and password. The +993 prefix stays fixed. Enter the eight local digits.
  2. Open Заказы and choose Создать заказ.
  3. Choose the region, enter the customer phone number and a positive price in TMT. These fields are required.
  4. Add an optional pickup, destination or note. On wide screens, use the large map beside the form. On smaller screens it sits below. Choose Откуда or Куда before placing a point; Убрать clears it.
  5. Choose a public announcement or a private driver, then send the order. For a private offer, a selected driver is required.

Public announcement

  1. Available immediatelyEligible drivers in every region can claim.
  2. Regional popupOnly the order’s region receives a 20-second popup.
  3. First claim winsThe order disappears for everyone else.

Private offer

  1. Select a driverOnline, free, fresh GPS, enough balance.
  2. 20-second offerThe selected driver must confirm.
  3. Expiry choiceOpen to all regions, or stop unassigned.
Choose whether to offer the order to other drivers after the private deadline. When ON, all regions open at once without a second popup or timer. When OFF, the order remains unassigned for the dispatcher to handle.

An early private decline frees the driver. The order stays hidden until its original deadline; the expiry choice then applies. The customer phone is hidden until acceptance.

Dispatcher · monitoring

Find a driver and follow the trip

  1. Open the monitoring map. Search the roster by name, phone or vehicle plate. Filter by region or status. On mobile, open the driver list as a bottom sheet.
  2. Select a driver from a map marker or roster row. Check the location age, online state and whether the driver is free.
  3. Use Предложить заказ for an eligible driver. Choose an existing waiting order or create a new private order.
  4. Follow the assigned order through pickup, waiting and the trip. A last known point is not a live position.

Drivers and balances

Create drivers in the driver list. Use a valid +993 phone number. A six-digit numeric password is allowed. When changing a phone or resetting a password, give the new credentials to the driver privately. Previous access is revoked and the driver signs in again.

Top up the pilot balance before assigning work. A driver needs the full order price to accept. For example, 15 TMT cannot cover a 30 TMT order. The app prevents a negative balance. If an adjustment response is uncertain, retry the same unchanged action. Refresh an old dashboard tab when asked; do not start a new adjustment until you check the balance history.

Driver · shift

Get ready and go online

  1. Open the driver app and sign in with your assigned phone number and password.
  2. After the first driver login, allow precise location, then optional notifications. Enable Android system location. Denied or dismissed prompts do not repeat on login: use GPS or Profile to recover manually. Notifications never block a shift.
  3. Check the location and connection indicator. If it is still checking, wait for a fresh position and dispatcher connection. If there is a warning, read its message.
  4. Press the Online switch. The app confirms the shift with the dispatcher before showing that you are live.
  5. Swiping the app away keeps the shift running. When finished, press Offline. Android Force stop blocks delivery until reopening.
  1. Location readyPermission, system GPS and a fresh position.
  2. Connection readyThe app can reach the dispatcher.
  3. Online confirmedThe dispatcher can offer new work.

Region cards: orders : drivers. The first number counts active orders; the second counts online drivers. Counts update live.

SituationWhat to do

No internetRestore Wi-Fi or mobile data. GPS alone cannot send updates or accept work.
Outside every regionYour real location stays visible as outside region. You may claim public orders immediately, subject to the normal eligibility checks.

GPS can stay green without internet. The app tries to recover the same shift for 60 seconds. If it fails, restore internet and tap Online again. Accepted trips stay assigned; offline locations keep their last update time.

On the target Android phone, background delivery must still be checked with the screen locked and battery restrictions enabled. A closed browser does not provide reliable background alerts.

Driver · orders

From acceptance to completion

  1. AcceptChoose arrival time and confirm. Price is charged once.
  2. At pickupPress Я на месте and wait for the customer.
  3. Customer boardedPress Начать поездку.
  4. Trip finishedPress Завершить поездку.
  1. The home grid shows every saved region. Each card shows unfinished orders first, then online drivers. Claimed trips stay in the order count. Open a region for orders still available to claim.
  2. A new regional or private offer can appear automatically. A private offer has a fixed 20-second deadline. Decline it if you cannot take it.
  3. Check the price and arrival time. Accept only when you can carry out the trip. A customer phone appears after acceptance; use it to confirm pickup.
  4. Use the price, call and next-trip controls above the region grid. Open the trip details for extra information or permitted cancellation.
  5. Complete the trip once it is finished. Completion does not charge the price again.
If another driver claims first, the order disappears. If you try at the same moment, the app reports that it is already taken. Only one driver can own the order.

Balance and cancellation

You need enough balance for the full price. With 30 TMT, you can accept a 30 TMT order. With 15 TMT, a 30 TMT order can be visible but cannot be accepted.

You can cancel only while waiting for the customer, after Я на месте. You cannot cancel immediately after acceptance or once the trip starts. A permitted cancellation refunds the original charge once. Do not repeatedly accept and cancel to test a live customer order.

If you lose internet, keep the accepted trip in mind and reconnect. Do not create another account or accept a second trip. If the trip cannot be completed in the app, contact the dispatcher.

Both roles · communication

Chat and recovery

  1. Open the conversation with the dispatcher or selected driver. Type a message and press Send. Enter sends; Shift+Enter adds a line.
  2. Read older messages inside the chat area. The app keeps your reading position when you scroll upward.
  3. If a send fails, the draft stays available. Retry it. The same retry does not create a duplicate message.
  4. After returning, let the app recover missed messages and the current order. If session verification fails, restore internet and press Повторить подключение; sign-in details stay saved.
  1. Connection lostThe last displayed information may be old.
  2. ReconnectThe app checks current server state.
  3. Work continuesThe accepted trip and balance remain consistent.

Dispatcher and driver · trip actions

Cancel or complete?

Cancellation means no drive took place. Completion means the drive was provided.

  1. AcceptedThe driver is on the way. The quoted price was charged.
  2. WaitingThe driver arrived and is waiting for the customer.
  3. StartedThe customer boarded. Ordinary cancellation ends.
  4. CompletedThe drive was provided. No extra debit or refund.
StageDriverDispatcher
UnassignedAccept an eligible offerCancel before acceptance. There is no charge to refund.
AcceptedCall the customer and go to pickup. No cancellation.Cancel if the drive will not happen. Refund the recorded charge once.
WaitingStart after boarding, or cancel if the drive will not happen.Cancel before the trip starts. Refund the recorded charge once.
StartedComplete when the drive finishes. Cancellation is blocked.If the completed drive is stuck, complete manually with a required reason. No refund or second debit.
Completed or cancelledReview the resultReview the history. Do not repeat a terminal action.

When a trip needs completion

  1. A started trip older than 15 minutes receives a warning. This is a reminder, not automatic completion.
  2. Call the driver. Confirm whether the customer is still in the car or the drive is finished.
  3. If the drive is finished and the driver cannot close it, choose Завершить вручную. Enter the actual reason.
  4. Confirm the completed status. The record keeps the same driver, original charge, dispatcher identity and reason.
Started trips cannot be cancelled. Exceptional refunds require a separate reviewed recovery process.

Driver and dispatcher · candidate changes

Profile, history and notifications

Review a previous order

  1. Open Profile and expand an order in history. Check its TMT price, status, region and date.
  2. When available, review pickup, destination, customer phone and call action, dispatcher notes and cancellation reason.
  3. Use the lifecycle times and order identifier to discuss the same record with the dispatcher. Missing route details stay unspecified; history does not repeat a charge.

Chat alerts, even off shift

Every signed-in Android installation of an approved account can receive chat alerts with GPS and shift off. Messages arrive through the live connection or FCM fallback. Reading synchronizes across devices and clears the corresponding alerts.

Profile has one notification switch for orders and chat. Enable it and Android notification permission to receive system alerts. Muting either never blocks a shift. Logout, blocking and credential changes revoke the old installation access.

Dispatchers see a banner, sender and message preview with a short sound in each dashboard tab outside the active conversation. Browser sound may require a prior click.

This page describes the candidate, not a guarantee about an older APK. Test locked-screen and background delivery on the Infinix Android 14. Force stop, unavailable networks and battery restrictions can block or delay delivery.

Yandex: a fixed direct request succeeds. Automatic address lookup stays disabled until the quota reset period and timezone are confirmed. GPS and saved region boundaries keep working.

Driver and dispatcher · candidate access rules

One account, one working phone

The dispatcher approves the driver account. An approved driver can sign in on any phone; there is no separate device approval or device-management screen.

  1. Sign in on another phoneBoth phones can stay signed in, view the account and use chat.
  2. One phone owns the shiftA second phone cannot go Online or control the working phone's location or trip progress.
  3. Switch the working phoneEnd the first shift with Offline or logout. If its connection is lost, wait for its 60-second availability to expire before starting on the second phone.

Off shift, GPS obtains only a local fix: no coordinate upload or address lookup. Each phone shows its own coordinates. Online sends the fresh fix and acquires the shift together. Offline stops reports even during an assigned trip; the dispatcher keeps the last point and its time.

If a phone is lost or access must stop

  1. The dispatcher chooses Sign out everywhere for that driver. This ends the shift and revokes all sessions.
  2. The assigned trip, password and balance remain. The driver signs in again to continue the assigned trip.
  3. For a lost phone, reset the password too. Sign out everywhere alone does not prevent a new login by someone who knows the password.
Signing in on another phone never transfers a shift automatically. The server rejects competing starts; refresh the state after a blocked attempt.

Protect access without changing a trip

Choose the account action for the situation. These controls do not cancel an order or move money.

ActionResult
Sign out everywhereEnds every session and the shift. Keeps approval, password, balance and assigned trip. The driver can sign in again.
Block temporarilyEnter a required reason. Access and the shift stop immediately, even during a trip. The trip stays assigned: contact the driver and resolve it separately. Unblock permits a new login.
Delete accountPermanent access removal; no Reactivate action. Resolve unfinished trips first. Old messages, balance records, trips and statistical attribution remain.
  1. For a lost phone, sign out everywhere and reset the password. A person who still knows the password could otherwise sign in again.
  2. Do not cancel a trip merely to clear a blocked account. Use the permitted order action only after checking what happened.
  3. A released phone number can submit a new application. It needs fresh approval and starts with zero balance and separate history.
Deleting an account is not a database reset. Old completed/cancelled orders and financial records remain in normal operation. Clearing an isolated test environment is separate maintenance.
